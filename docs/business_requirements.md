# Business Requirements

## Business Context

Northstar Retail is a multi-category retail company that sells products across multiple customer segments and geographic regions.

The company collects transactional sales data but currently lacks a centralized business intelligence solution for monitoring performance and identifying trends.

Leadership wants a reporting system that provides a clear view of sales, profitability, customer behaviour, and product performance to support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem

Business stakeholders currently rely on raw transactional data and fragmented reporting, making it difficult to answer important questions such as:

- How is revenue changing over time?
- Which products and categories generate the most profit?
- Which customer segments are most valuable?
- Which regions are performing well or poorly?
- Are discounts negatively affecting profitability?
- Are there seasonal patterns in sales?

The goal of this project is to develop an end-to-end analytics solution that transforms raw transactional data into structured business insights.

## Project Objective

Develop a centralized business intelligence platform using Python, SQL, dimensional data modelling, and Power BI to enable stakeholders to:

- Monitor key business performance indicators
- Analyze sales and profitability trends
- Evaluate product and category performance
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour
- Compare regional performance
- Identify potential business risks and opportunities

## Stakeholders

### Executive Leadership
Needs a high-level view of overall business performance, including revenue, profit, growth, and major trends.

### Sales Management
Needs to evaluate sales performance across products, categories, customer segments, and regions.

### Product Management
Needs to understand which products and categories are driving revenue and profitability.

### Marketing / Customer Strategy
Needs insight into customer segments, purchasing behaviour, and high-value customers.

## Key Business Questions

### Sales Performance
1. How has total revenue changed over time?
2. What are the strongest and weakest sales periods?
3. How does current performance compare with previous periods?
4. Which regions generate the most revenue?

### Profitability
5. Which products and categories generate the most profit?
6. Which products generate high revenue but low profit?
7. How do discounts affect profitability?
8. Are any categories or regions consistently underperforming?

### Customer Analytics
9. Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
10. Who are the highest-value customers?
11. What is the average order value by customer segment?
12. How does purchasing behaviour differ across customer groups?

### Product Analytics
13. Which products and categories generate the most sales?
14. Which products have the highest and lowest margins?
15. Are certain products experiencing declining performance?
16. What proportion of revenue is generated by top-performing products?

## Key Performance Indicators

### Revenue
Total value of sales generated during a selected period.

### Total Profit
Total profit generated from sales.

### Profit Margin
Profit expressed as a percentage of revenue.

Formula:

Profit Margin = Total Profit / Total Revenue

### Total Orders
Number of unique customer orders.

### Average Order Value
Average revenue generated per order.

Formula:

Average Order Value = Total Revenue / Total Orders

### Total Customers
Number of unique customers who placed orders.

### Revenue Growth
Percentage change in revenue compared with the previous period.

### Year-over-Year Revenue Growth
Percentage change in revenue compared with the same period in the previous year.

### Average Discount
Average discount applied to transactions.
